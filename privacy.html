Drone for Subsonic: Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Drone is a music player for music servers that you or someone you trust run, such as Navidrome or other Subsonic-compatible servers. Drone does not collect, sell or share your personal data.

What Drone stores on your device

  • Your server address and username, and a salted authentication token in the iOS Keychain. Your password is never stored.
  • A cache of your library, playlists, artwork, play history and queue so the app opens fast.
  • Songs you choose to download for offline listening.

All of this stays on your device. Signing out or deleting the app removes it.

Who Drone talks to

Drone connects only to the music server you sign in to. It sends that server the requests it needs to browse, search, stream, star and scrobble music. That server is run by you or its owner, not by us, and its own privacy practices apply.

Drone has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not operate any server that receives your data.

Siri

If you use Siri with Drone, Apple handles your voice request under Apple's privacy policy. Drone receives only the name of what you asked to play and looks it up on your music server.

Children

Drone does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
